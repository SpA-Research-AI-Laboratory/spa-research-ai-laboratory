03 / Related SpA lesson

A reproducible number still needs the right meaning.

Suppose a paper defines radiographic progression as a change greater than 2 units. The mathematical complement is a change less than or equal to 2, not strictly less than 2. A value exactly equal to 2 changes category if that boundary is copied incorrectly. This is why testing arithmetic, preserving the original definition and interpreting the endpoint are separate jobs.

The PREVENT report, Two-year imaging outcomes from a phase 3 randomized trial of secukinumab in patients with non-radiographic axial spondyloarthritis, reports spinal progression greater than 2 mSASSS units alongside a separate no-progression threshold based on the smallest detectable change, 0.76. mSASSS is a radiographic spinal-damage score. These definitions answer different classification questions; they should not be silently substituted for one another. Original full-text report.

Inflammatory activity, prevention of additional structural progression and repair of existing damage remain distinct. A non-progression classification does not itself demonstrate repair. Reproducing a classification cannot turn it into evidence of a different outcome. The bounded P002 audit found no lesion-specific repair endpoint in its inspected material; it did not prove repair impossible or exhaust the literature.

Concept check: If a script reproduces every published percentage, is its repair claim validated? No. Numerical reproducibility must still be paired with a suitable endpoint, a defensible design and source-supported interpretation.
