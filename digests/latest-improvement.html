Can we inspect the checks behind a completion claim?

This improvement review examines one real use of the lab's existing scientific-verification procedure. The useful result is a traceable account of what was checked, repaired and left unknown. It does not establish that the procedure generally saves time or prevents scientific errors.

CEO assessment

The lab needs to distinguish a finished piece of work from a persuasive label. A task can produce a report while its original scientific question remains unanswered. The existing D063 procedure addresses that risk by keeping the original criteria visible, linking important claims to inspectable evidence, and preserving failed checks alongside repairs.

For this cycle, I selected one retrospective audit of the completed G014 NFKBIZ verification stage. This was a real opportunity to observe the procedure; creating another tool or rerunning an old analysis would have added activity without answering whether the current records were useful. The scientific results already existed when this case was selected. This is an outcome-exposed retrospective assessment, not a prospectively registered experiment comparing review methods.

The decision is to retain the existing procedure within its present scope and continue finite scientific work. No department, schedule or review authority changes. Broader verification machinery, another graph build, a new correction-preview run and scheduler redesign remain deferred. The previous I008 correction-preview issue remains open; a limited scan of new records has not established a new ordinary application. That is not a new full census or evidence that the preview is ineffective.

What this review actually found

G014 concerns a finite NFKBIZ gene-activity comparison in seven rheumatoid-arthritis/osteoarthritis donors from one study. Its completion does not complete the broader genomics programme or establish an axial-SpA finding. This cycle inspected existing records and repeated the read-only certificate and endpoint checks; it did not calculate new donor responses or rerun source research.

The record accounts for all five original criteria and seven frozen checks. The scientific definitions and reference checks were frozen in the earlier custom contract before the actual response extraction. The later conversion into the lab's native certificate format is explicitly retrospective. Both current record gates returned successfully. This supports the recorded finite-scope completion and presentation readiness at their stated internal scope; the gates do not establish biomedical truth.

Earlier synthetic controls exposed actual defects in draft code: it failed to reject a deliberately duplicated donor, changed its interpretation when donor rows were reordered, and produced a different result when laboratory batch labels were represented as numbers rather than text. These tests used constructed inputs; the original G012 challenge did not read the expression-data file. They do not show that real study donors were duplicated or that D063 later prevented those errors. Later helper mistakes concern how verification records were assembled; they are not evidence that two independent numerical calculations disagreed. A repaired record must still satisfy the original check against its original reference.

The native attempt log is empty, while the linked historical inventory preserves earlier failures. Empty therefore means no events in that particular log, not a failure-free project. Exact bytes and precise times for some failed helper versions are unknown. Those gaps remain disclosed rather than reconstructed as observed facts.

The G014 stage lasted about 48 minutes 22 seconds between its recorded start and finish. That is total elapsed stage time, including other work and interruptions. It cannot be reported as the cost of D063, active labour or a saving. One selected case cannot establish a general reliability improvement, an error-prevention rate or lower founder burden.

Completion, delivery and writing remain separate. The finite scientific scope is complete and its endpoint is checked. The slideshow is queued; display and reading have not been observed. Manuscript/media writing remains unauthorized. Central registration is administrative follow-through; it cannot supply a display receipt or writing grant.

SpA research lesson: a check must match the claim

A measurement answers a question about what it measured. A gene-activity assay may support a bounded statement about that assay in its sampled cells. It cannot by itself answer whether a treatment helps people or whether established spinal damage reverses. Each broader claim needs evidence measuring the corresponding outcome. The same discipline applies to project management: completing a calculation stage does not automatically complete a wider research programme.
